4-Day Split

Mon · Tue · Wed · Fri — same lifts each week. Core finisher on Mon & Wed.

MONTUEWEDFRICORE
4×10–12 = 4 sets of 10–12 reps. Superset = alternate the moves, rest after the pair. Reps in reserve = stop with that many reps left in the tank. Pick weights that land you in the rep range — no numbers listed on purpose.
